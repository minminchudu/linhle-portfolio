Competitor Gap — What Users Say Rivals Do Better
TikTok
The user-cited gold standard for content variety and algorithm responsiveness
"The TikTok For You Page actually learns what you like. Every swipe feels different. Instagram gives me the same five topics on repeat." ResetEra · 2024
"TikTok keeps its video in the video section. It doesn't invade my DMs or my main feed." X / Twitter · 2025
What Instagram is missing
Variety per session and surface containment. Users don't object to Instagram having a short-form video tab — they object to it bleeding across the whole app. TikTok doesn't do that.
Sources: ResetEra, X / Twitter, Vice, Morning Consult Q1 2025
Snapchat
Cited specifically for preserving the friend-graph experience Instagram abandoned
"Snap still feels like it's actually for my friends. Instagram used to feel that way." Quora · 2024
"BeReal and Snapchat offer the unedited, mundane content that Instagram lost." Cosmopolitan India · 2024
What Instagram is missing
Friend-graph preservation. Snapchat never deprioritised who you know for what you watch. Morning Consult Q1 2025 places Snapchat ahead of YouTube Shorts among Gen Z daily users — driven by this distinction.
Sources: Quora, Cosmopolitan India, Morning Consult Q1 2025
YouTube Shorts
Less aggressive surface invasion — short-form and long-form coexist without cannibalising each other
"Shorts are aggressive but at least I can still find my subscriptions immediately. Instagram buried my follows." X / Twitter · 2025
"YouTube Shorts feels less aggressive than Reels partly because the main YouTube app still surfaces subscriptions and search prominently." User sentiment synthesis · PiunikaWeb / ResetEra 2025
What Instagram is missing
A clear separation between short-form and the rest of the product. On YouTube, short-form is one surface. On Instagram, Reels is everywhere — friend-feed, DM swipe, Explore — with no clear zone.
Sources: X / Twitter, PiunikaWeb, ResetEra · 2025
