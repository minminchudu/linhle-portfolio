Appendix — AI Auditor Challenge
Appendix A
Challenge rebuttal

"Alpha=0.15 improves margin but hurts new user conversion. New users have 2.3× LTV. Launch anyway?"

Premise 1
"Alpha=0.15 improves margin"

Arm B raw delta: −$0.698/impr (p=0.504). Non-delayed: −$0.526/impr (p=0.641). No confirmed margin improvement. Nothing on one side of the scale.

Premise 2
"Alpha=0.15 hurts new user conversion"

Arm B total CVR: 4.570% vs Baseline 3.809% — CVR is higher, not lower. New-visitor specific: Arm B 4.64% vs Baseline 4.68% — a 0.04pp difference below noise threshold.

Premise 3
"2.3× LTV therefore launch"

Requires a confirmed negative CVR to have leverage. 2.3× applied to 0.04pp = 0.09pp weighted effect — below measurement precision. No trade-off to compute. The real G1 concern is Arm C, which has a quantifiable answer.

Why small sample size does not save the arm

  • Small samples produce wide confidence intervals — making it harder to detect a guardrail failure, not easier
  • Even the most favourable uncertainty estimate (CI upper = 3.27%) remains below the floor (3.3146%) — the breach is not a point-estimate artifact
  • Formal test: z=−2.249 · p=0.0123 — significant at conventional thresholds
  • Pre-registered in Week 5 at Wilson CI lower bound of Baseline CVR — derived before results were seen

The guardrail can be revised by a documented business decision before the follow-on experiment begins. It cannot be widened retroactively to pass Arm C on this experiment's data.

Appendix — Power Position
Appendix C
Why p=0.563 is not "probably zero"

The experiment was 12× underpowered in effective comparisons for the observed signal

ParameterRequiredActual

Gap—12× in effective comparisons

Why p=0.563 is expected:

At 12× underpowering, a genuine +$0.63/impr effect would produce p-values in the 0.4–0.7 range the majority of the time. p=0.563 is not evidence the effect is zero — it is the predictable outcome of running with one-twelfth the required resolution.

The shared control group of 5,041 observations constrains every arm comparison — arm impressions (~15,000 each) are not the bottleneck; the control pool is.

The signal emerged under trimming: Arm C p=0.0001 positive. Once delay-driven extreme observations are removed, the effect surfaces with formal statistical significance. The experiment is telling us the signal is real but obscured — not absent.
