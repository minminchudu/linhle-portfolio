Metric | Value | Formula
Discount Rate (WACC) | 0.1 | ASSUMPTION: deliberate hurdle rate, not Spotify's WACC. Analyst WACC estimates for Spotify run ≈6.5–7.3% (GuruFocus 7.26%, Jul 2026; Alpha Spread 6.47%) — but company WACC prices the AVERAGE Spotify cash flow; an unproven initiative warrants a project risk premium. Empirics: firms' hurdle rates average ~15% vs WACCs near 8% (Jagannathan, Matsa, Meier & Tarhan 2016, J. Financial Economics), so 10% ≈ WACC +300bp is a MILD premium by documented practice. Materiality is low by construction: all three annual net flows are positive (build absorbed inside Year 1), so NPV is positive at ANY rate — €16.3M at 10% vs €17.2M at 7.3% (±5%). The rate is chosen for credibility; the case is decided by lift, not discounting.
